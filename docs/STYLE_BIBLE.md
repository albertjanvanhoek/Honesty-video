# Style Bible

Source: the mood board (`docs/moodboard.webp`) and the music: frenchcore at ~207 BPM. The aim is **uplifting, energy-giving and spacious**.

## The journey

The film travels from a rainy night to a golden sunrise, following the song's turn from defending yourself to changing well:

| time | light | music | lyrics |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:50 | rainy night, city skyline | intro and build | "People say: I am honest" … sport |
| 0:50–1:20 | storm, lightning on the hardest kicks | drop A | "You were wrong" … "defend" / "change" |
| 1:20–1:45 | blue hour, mist on the lake | breakdown | "closer to true" … "a compass is not faithful" |
| 1:45–2:25 | dawn to sunrise, a warm burst as it lands | drop B | "frozen needle" … "Turn" … "Teach me, again" |
| 2:25–2:42 | golden hour | break, final lines | "how you become / how you come home" |
| 2:42–3:05 | golden hour; the figure on the cliff | instrumental outro | then the title card |

## Balance

- **Lyrics are the hero:** big, readable, synced per word.
- **The landscape gives space:** wide, layered ridges and a lake, with the camera gliding faster when the music has more energy.

## Palette

The swatch strip of the mood board: charcoal `#172429`, slate `#222627`, stone `#71716f`, sand `#9e8f7f`, ochre `#bb8e5e`, amber `#cd924f`, peach `#e39c74`. Plus paper `#e9e1d2` and cream `#f4ece0` for notes and type, brass for the compass. Source of truth: `PALETTE` in `app/src/look.ts`.

## Typography

- **Cormorant Garamond** 700 for the lyrics: a tall, cinematic serif lit softly from behind. Quoted speech in italic.
- **Spaced capitals** for the title card: "HONESTY IS A COMPASS / truth / turning / growth / courage / coming home".
- **Caveat** handwriting for the torn-paper notes.
- Karaoke: unsung cream (dimmed), the sung word amber, sung cream. Rigid lines ("never changes", "only stuck") in frost.

## Elements

- **The compass** (black face, brass case, as on the mood board) rises at "You play honesty"; its needle is a spring-damper model driven by the lyrics: knocked, pinned, frozen, turned, and always swinging back to north.
- **Torn-paper notes**, taped, written word by word as they are sung: the sport notebook; the journal ("Defend myself / Blame / Ignore / Stay the same" crossed out → "Tell the truth / Choose change / Be better / Come home"); margin notes.
- **A silhouetted figure**: walking through the rain at the start; standing on a cliff, arms opening to the sunrise, in the outro.

## Frenchcore

The kick is the heartbeat, and it is felt, not shown as effects for their own sake:

- in the drops the whole frame punches on each kick, the hardest kicks give a small shake, and the light lifts;
- each lyric word slams in on its beat;
- rain jolts on the kick; lightning on the storm's hardest kicks;
- a warm burst of light as each drop lands;
- the intro and breakdown stay calm, so the drops land harder.
